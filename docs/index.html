Tell

Tell is the instrument built for the first empirical test of an original theory, Recursive Information-Container Dynamics (RICD): a formal model of how far an institution's official, operational and meta layers of information diverge, how the cost of keeping up that divergence accumulates as resource debt, and how an outside anchor moves the point of no return. Tracker v3 passed its pre-registered test (32 of 56 closures caught, Youden's J 0.49), beat every baseline tracker, and catches closures that size and sector miss. This page applies the frozen tracker v3 to every U.S. four-year degree-granting college on federal data for fiscal years 2014 to 2024.

How to read a flag. A flag marks risk of closure: the college's records over the last eleven years resemble those of colleges that went on to close. It is not a forecast that the college will close, and the score is not a probability of closure. Many flagged colleges do not close: some are carried by anchors the federal records only partly show, or turn around. Anchors matter: of the closed colleges whose anchoring could be measured, none was anchored when its trouble began.

What each column means

Reading is the tracker's verdict after its anchors are counted. Flagged means the v3 score is at or above the frozen cut point and the college's anchors do not bring it below. Flag cleared by anchors means v3 flagged the college but its anchors (federal, state and local appropriations, gifts, membership in a public or nonprofit system) carry it below the cut, as RICD's anchor sensor predicts for anchored institutions. Not flagged means the v3 score is below the cut. Not scorable means the college's federal record is too short or too irregular to read, with the reason given.

v3 score is the frozen classifier's output from the divergence between the college's official and operational records, its resource debt and reserves, the slide in its cost coverage toward break-even, drift and jumps in its enrollment, its reporting gaps, and its size and sector. After anchors is the same score once the anchor sensor's frozen coefficients are applied; anchoring can only lower it. Anchors counted shows the anchor inputs: appropriations and gifts as shares of spending (fiscal 2022 to 2024), and system membership.

Scope: four-year degree-granting colleges, the frame tracker v3 was trained and tested on; two-year colleges are not scored. Scores are redone once a year, when a new federal finance year is released. These are interim scores; the blind retest is committed separately before the next header file appears. Method, protocols and every result: the repository (live tracker protocol).

The original prototype dashboard (tracker v1, retired) is kept on record at v1.html.
Data: IPEDS (NCES) finance, enrollment and institutional characteristics files; College Scorecard.
